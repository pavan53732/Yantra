# Glossary

- Workspace: the durable operating context for repository work.
- Mission: a bounded engineering goal with planning, approval, execution, and verification.
- Agent: a specialised execution role working within mission and governance constraints.
- Verification: the evidence required before work is considered complete.
- Knowledge: approved repository context used for grounding and retrieval.
- Memory: retained context that supports continuity across missions and workspaces.
