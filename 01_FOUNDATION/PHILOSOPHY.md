# Philosophy

Yantra is an engineering operating system, not a chat-first application. It exists to make complex software work more governable, reviewable, and continuous through a workspace-first, mission-first product model.

## Philosophical constraints

- Human-supervised autonomy beats uncontrolled automation.
- Clean workflows matter more than exposed internal complexity.
- Architecture integrity matters more than short-term convenience.
- Repository quality is part of product quality.
