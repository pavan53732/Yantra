# Design System

## Visual posture

Yantra should be dark-first, clean, spacious, restrained, and product-like rather than tool-heavy. Typography, spacing, hierarchy, and subtle motion should do most of the work instead of visual clutter.[cite:18]

## Component guidance

- Prefer cards and focused surfaces over crowded multi-panel layouts.
- Keep top-level navigation limited and stable.
- Push secondary controls into contextual views.
- Use subtle Framer Motion transitions sparingly.
- Maintain minimal noise and high readability throughout the app.[cite:18]
